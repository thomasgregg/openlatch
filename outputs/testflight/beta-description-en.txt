OpenLatch releases a compatible Tesla's driver door over local Bluetooth.
Pair a key directly in the vehicle, then use the app or an Apple Shortcut.
Supports multiple cars, English and German. No Tesla account login or backend.

This is an early hardware beta. Real-vehicle and firmware compatibility remain
under evaluation. The app disconnects when backgrounded; it is not a passive
phone-key replacement. A Tesla key card is required for initial authorization.
OpenLatch is an independent project and is not affiliated with Tesla.
